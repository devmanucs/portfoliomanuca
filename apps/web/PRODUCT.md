# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, product teams and potential clients evaluating Manuella's front-end and UI/UX work. They need to understand her capabilities, process and outcomes quickly, then reach her or review a detailed case.

## Product Purpose

Present Manuella Carvalho as a developer-designer who joins product thinking, interface craft and implementation. Success means visitors can identify her fit, inspect credible project evidence and contact her without friction. The private admin keeps the portfolio and résumé current from one source of truth.

## Brand Personality

Artesanal, tátil e tecnicamente confiante. A experiência mistura a materialidade de madeira e metal escovado, a expressividade gráfica brasileira e a clareza de um produto bem construído. Deve parecer pessoal e viva, nunca genérica ou estereotipada.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark IDE portfolios with neon gradients and glowing glass cards.
- Loud startup landing pages with oversized bold headings and excessive motion.
- Decorative editorial styling that obscures project evidence.
- Template-like grids of identical cards, repeated section eyebrows and gratuitous shadows.
- Multiple competing accent colors or timeline colors used as generic status colors.
- Rosa como atalho automático para feminilidade.
- Portfólios neutros e monocromáticos que apagam a personalidade da autora.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work before describing the persona.
2. Let technical precision and editorial restraint create confidence.
3. Use espresso como ação principal e cobre/bronze como acentos narrativos.
4. Keep project evidence, code surfaces and outcomes easy to scan.
5. Preserve calm pacing while making every interaction accessible and responsive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast for body and placeholder text, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, 40–44px touch targets where practical and reduced-motion alternatives for every non-essential animation.
